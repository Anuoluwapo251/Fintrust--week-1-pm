# Reporting Plan

- **Cadence:** Brief status check-in at the end of each week (Weeks 2–4), summarizing progress, blockers, and updated risks/dependencies.
- **Format:** Short written status report (1 page) covering: completed activities, upcoming activities, active risks, active dependencies, and any escalations.
- **Trigger-based updates:** Any track flags PM immediately if a deliverable will be late or a dependency is at risk, rather than waiting for the weekly cadence.
- **Week 3 checkpoint:** Additional mid-integration review to confirm all tracks' outputs are on track to integrate cleanly by Week 4.
- **Week 4 closeout:** Final report summarizing delivery against the Charter's success criteria, plus lessons learned.
