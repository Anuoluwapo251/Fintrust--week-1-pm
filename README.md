# FinTrust Digital Bank — Week 1 (Project Management Track)

**Programme:** AnalystLab Africa — Experience Lab Internship
**Project:** FinTrust Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution
**Track:** Project Management
**Week:** 1 of 4 — Understand & Plan

## Overview

FinTrust Digital Bank is a fictional digital bank seeking to use data, machine learning, and generative AI to better understand customer/transaction behaviour, support risk-related decision-making, improve operational processes, and strengthen digital customer support.

Over four weeks, four technical tracks — Data Analytics, Data Science, ML Engineering, and Generative AI — build toward one integrated solution:

```
Customer & Transaction Data
        → Analytics Findings & KPIs
        → Predictive Risk Intelligence
        → ML Workflow/Service
        → Knowledge-Based GenAI Support
        → Integrated FinTrust Solution
```

As the **Project Management** track, my role is to coordinate that integration — not to build a track-specific technical output, but to define scope, sequence dependencies, track risk, and keep delivery on schedule across all four tracks.

Week 1 was completed individually, with no formal cross-track collaboration requirement. Formal collaboration begins in Week 2.
